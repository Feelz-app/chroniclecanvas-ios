Delete your account without waiting on support.

Sign in with the account you want to remove, optionally tell us why you're leaving, and delete it yourself in one pass. If the account owns a paid Chronicle Canvas plan, the subscription is canceled before the account disappears.

Direct self-serve account deletion

Step 1

Sign in to the account you want to delete

This page works even if the app has already been removed from your phone. Use the exact Chronicle Canvas email and password for the account you want gone.

Need a lighter touch instead? Use the Delete Data page to remove only specific parts of the account.

Step 2

Delete the account

You are signed in to the exact account that will be removed.

Signed in as: Not signed in

This permanently removes the sign-in and the matching Chronicle Canvas data. Shared members only delete their own access. Paid owners also cancel the Chronicle Canvas subscription tied to that account.

What gets removed

Your Chronicle Canvas sign-in and the account data linked to it. For paid owners, that includes the workspace data under that account. For shared members, it removes the member account and shared access.

What to use instead

If you only want a partial cleanup, use the Delete Data page to wipe specific things like timeline events, trash, files, or layout preferences without deleting the whole account.
