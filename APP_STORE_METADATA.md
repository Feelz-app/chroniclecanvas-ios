## Description

Chronicle Canvas is a flexible timeline workspace for the records and details people actually need to keep close.

Use it to organize milestones, family records, vehicle documents, home projects, reminders, attachments, and everyday paperwork in one place. Build a timeline that fits your life, then shape the details so the workspace feels personal instead of rigid.

With paid plans, you can also share access with authorized users while keeping quiet control over what they can see. Share the files someone needs without exposing the events they do not.

### What you can do in Chronicle Canvas

- Track milestones, reminders, notes, and custom details
- Organize records in folders, subfolders, and mini events
- Upload attachments like photos, receipts, and documents
- Share access with family or trusted users on paid plans
- Hide selected events or folders from specific shared users
- Keep vehicle, property, medical, school, or household records in one place

### Memberships

Chronicle Canvas offers optional auto-renewing Apple subscriptions for premium features inside the iPhone app.

- Home monthly membership
- Advanced monthly membership

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings after purchase.

## Review Notes

Chronicle Canvas uses Apple In-App Purchase for memberships on iPhone and keeps Stripe on the web version only.

Test flow:

1. Sign into the app with a Chronicle Canvas account.
2. Open the plan panel.
3. Choose Home or Advanced.
4. The iPhone wrapper routes the purchase through Apple subscriptions.
5. The app syncs the resulting entitlement back into the same Chronicle Canvas account.
